# 2026-11-01 — Home page: My top skills

## Decided
- The new design of 2026-10-31 replaces the Skills explorer on the home page with a static block, "My top skills". Search and filters move to a separately designed `/skills` page.
- `skills.top` selects the skills in the block (owner's decision, the handoff called it `featured`). No limit: every marked skill is shown.
- `getSkills` stays unused until the `/skills` page, and the Experience links stay on `/projects` until the Projects page (owner's decisions).
- The starting set is the eight skills from the design with their current levels; the owner corrects the data in the admin. GraphQL, Jest and Tailwind show the placeholder level 6 until then.
- The meter runs from 1 to 10, and the bar and the value always share a line, unlike the prototype (owner's decisions).
