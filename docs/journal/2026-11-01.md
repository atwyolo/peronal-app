# 2026-11-01 — Home page: My top skills and Blog

## Decided
- The new design of 2026-10-31 replaces the Skills explorer on the home page with a static block, "My top skills". Search and filters move to a separately designed `/skills` page.
- `skills.top` selects the skills in the block (owner's decision, the handoff called it `featured`). No limit: every marked skill is shown.
- `getSkills` stays unused until the `/skills` page, and the Experience links stay on `/projects` until the Projects page (owner's decisions).
- The starting set is the eight skills from the design with their current levels; the owner corrects the data in the admin. GraphQL, Jest and Tailwind show the placeholder level 6 until then.
- The meter runs from 1 to 10, and the bar and the value always share a line, unlike the prototype (owner's decisions).

## Done
- `SkillRow`, `TopSkills`, `getTopSkills`; the block replaces the dump's Skills section.
- A Skills item in the header and the mobile menu, leading to the 404 page until the page exists.
- Production database, confirmed by the owner: `skills.top` added and eight rows marked in one transaction. 8 of 42 skills are marked.

## Notes
- `next build` type-checks test files too, so a test importing a component that does not exist yet stops the e2e run before any test starts.
- The first e2e run of the block passed entirely, so it proved nothing. Deliberately broken builds then failed the tests for the total, the order, the columns, the row layout, the link and the page width.

## Blog block
The design of 2026-11-01 added a topic badge before the date and handoffs for every other page. The re-export restored `featured` and the old mobile skill row in the home handoff; the owner's decisions are back in it.

- Topics are `post_topics` and `post_topic_translations`, and `posts.topic_id` is required (owner's decision). The Blog handoff's enum with labels in messages is not used: the owner edits topics in the admin, and the Blog filter shows topics with no posts. Counts come from a join, nothing stores them.
- All eight locales are filled, so `/ru` shows a Russian badge next to English content until the posts are translated.
- Production database, confirmed by the owner: both tables and `posts.topic_id` in one transaction. 3 topics, 24 labels, counts Technical 2, Software 1, Hobby 0.
- `PostCard`, `Blog`, the UTC time zone in the i18n config; the last dump and `Footer.copyright` are gone.

## Notes on the Blog block

- A deliberately broken build showed a gap: without `lg:self-end` the link stretches and its right edge still matches the section, so the test now checks the width too.
